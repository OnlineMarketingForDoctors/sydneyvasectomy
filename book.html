What happens next

From booking to all clear

Five steps, spread over about three months. Only one of them involves the procedure room.

  1. Book

    Choose a date in the calendar and pay the $100 deposit. Your time is held.

  2. Sign

    Three days out we text you the consent form. Read it, sign it on your phone.

  3. Prepare

    Stop blood thinners a week before. Shave on the morning. Plan the week after if your job is physical.

  4. Come in

    Consultation, then the procedure — about an hour in the building. You drive yourself home.

  5. Confirm

    A semen analysis at around three months, using the pathology form we give you. We confirm the all clear in writing.

All patient information

Do I need a GP referral?

No. Book directly.

Can I have the consult and procedure on the same day?

Yes — that's how most bookings run. The consultation happens first, and if everything is in order the procedure follows in the same visit.

What does it cost?

$830, less a $233 Medicare rebate — $597 out of pocket. A $100 deposit secures your appointment and comes off the total. Full breakdown →

Can I change my appointment?

Yes. Call the practice as early as you can and we'll move it.

Will I be able to drive home?

Yes. It's done under local anaesthetic, so you can drive yourself home afterwards.

How long should I take off work?

If your job doesn't involve heavy lifting, you can go back straight away. If it does, plan a week of light duties. We can write a medical certificate.
