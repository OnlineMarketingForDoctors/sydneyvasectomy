Dr Geoff Cashion

MBBS (University of Queensland) · FACRRM · FRCEM

Born in Brisbane, raised in Rockhampton, and a graduate of the University of Queensland in 2002. Dr Cashion spent years in emergency medicine and general practice before completing no-scalpel vasectomy training under Dr Doug Stein in Florida — the surgeon who taught much of the world the technique — with further training in Australia.

He now performs more than 30 vasectomies a week across Australia, and over 4,000 a year: more no-scalpel vasectomies annually than any other doctor in the country.

He is a Fellow of the Australian College of Rural and Remote Medicine and of the Royal College of Emergency Medicine, and teaches general practice registrars as a GP Supervisor with James Cook University.

Geoff lives in Sydney with his wife and two daughters. Outside medicine he is a professional recording artist, with two albums released and a national tour behind him.

Vasectomist since 2008

Dr Matt Valentine

MBBS (University of Adelaide) · FRACGP

Dr Valentine served five years as a Medical Officer with the Royal Australian Air Force before moving into general practice. He trained first in the traditional vasectomy technique, then travelled to the United States to specialise in the no-scalpel method.

Vasectomy has been his full-time medical practice for over fifteen years. He has performed more than 17,000 vasectomies, and currently does over 250 every month.
