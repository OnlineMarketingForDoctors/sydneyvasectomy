After your procedure

Four ways to claim your rebate

If you've paid in full and we haven't submitted the claim for you, you make the Medicare claim yourself. It takes a couple of minutes. Pick whichever of these suits you.

Upload your invoice to your Medicare online account

Sign in through myGov, choose Make a claim, and upload a copy of the paid invoice we email you. The rebate goes to the bank account Medicare has on file.

Open Medicare online accounts

Claim on the Express Plus Medicare app

The fastest option if you already have the app. Download it, sign in with your myGov details, and process the claim from your phone with a photo of the invoice.

Get the Express Plus app

Complete a form and post it

Fill in a Medicare claim form (MS014), attach your paid invoice, and send it in. Slower than the other options, but it works without a myGov account.

Download form MS014

Take it into a service centre

Bring your paid invoice and your Medicare card to any Services Australia service centre and they'll process the claim over the counter.

Find a service centre

Whichever route you take, you need a copy of your invoice listing the services received and confirming the account is paid in full. We email it within 48 hours of your appointment — if it hasn't arrived, write to info@sydneyvasectomy.com.au.

Contact us

Can I use my private health insurance?

No. We perform vasectomies in medical centres rather than private hospitals, and private health cover only applies when a urologist operates in a hospital or day surgery.

It almost certainly works out cheaper with us anyway — see the comparison above.

When is the deposit due?

The $100 deposit is taken when you book, and it secures your appointment time. It comes off the total — the remaining balance is due on the day.

How do I pay the balance?

On the day of the procedure, at the practice. Once it's paid in full you'll be given a receipt, and the invoice follows by email within 48 hours.

How long does the Medicare rebate take?

Claims made through your online account or the Express Plus app are usually paid into your nominated bank account within a few days. Postal claims take longer.

Do I need a GP referral to get the rebate?

No. A GP referral isn't needed to book, and it isn't needed to claim.

Is the consultation charged separately?

No. The consultation, the procedure, your post-operative support and the pathology request form for your semen analysis are all covered by the one fee.
